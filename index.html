About Me

I'm Ujjwal Singh, a Computer Science graduate from Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology (MMMUT) and current M.Tech student at National Institute of Technology Surathkal (NIT Surathkal). I am actively building a strong foundation in Machine Learning and Data Science from mathematical first principles, developing practical projects, and sharing my notes and insights through technical blogs.

Why Machine Learning?

Machine Learning caught my attention during my B.Tech, but I wanted to understand the underlying mathematics rather than treating algorithms as black boxes. During my GATE preparation, studying Linear Algebra led me to Professor Gilbert Strang's MIT OpenCourseWare lectures, which sparked my interest in understanding how intelligent systems work.

Today, I focus on learning the fundamentals from first principles, practicing implementations from scratch, and documenting my progress through code and writing.
